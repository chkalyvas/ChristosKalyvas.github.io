## About Me

I work where AI systems meet adversaries: securing the models, agents and pipelines organisations now depend on, and using AI to make cyber defence faster and more anticipatory. My background spans enterprise cyber defence, large-scale data engineering and a decade of academic research in machine learning and multi-objective optimisation.

In industry I have authored an organisation-wide AI security plan, built AI asset visibility and shadow-AI detection, red-teamed LLM guardrails before adoption, and run reliability and security for a large-scale vulnerability intelligence service. I hold a PhD in Intelligent Big Data Management, an MSc in Information and Communication Systems Security and a BSc in Mathematics from the University of the Aegean, with 7 publications and more than 170 citations.
