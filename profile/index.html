About Me

I work where AI systems meet adversaries: securing the models, agents and pipelines organisations now depend on, and using AI to make cyber defence faster and more anticipatory. My background spans enterprise cyber defence, large-scale data engineering and a decade of academic research in machine learning and multi-objective optimisation.

In industry I have shaped AI security planning and controls, built AI asset visibility and shadow-AI detection, red-teamed LLM guardrails before adoption, and worked on the reliability and security of vulnerability intelligence platforms. I hold a PhD in Intelligent Big Data Management, an MSc in Information and Communication Systems Security and a BSc in Mathematics from the University of the Aegean, with 7 publications and about 150 citations.

Views expressed on this site are my own and do not represent any employer, client or organisation I work with.

📚 My Research

I work on the security of AI systems and on AI for cyber defence: AI security plans and controls, shadow-AI detection, prompt-injection testing and guardrail evaluation, and agentic AI threat modelling.

Alongside this I work on vulnerability intelligence, and my academic background is in machine learning and multi-objective (skyline / Pareto) optimisation over big data.

Working on AI security, LLM red-teaming or vulnerability intelligence? I’m always glad to compare notes, review ideas or collaborate on research. Say hello at christos@christoskalyvas.eu.
